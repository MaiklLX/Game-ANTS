## Scope

Add compact, state-aware field tips to Meadow levels 002–005 while leaving the first-session tutorial on level 001 unchanged.

- **002 — Two Colors, One Slot:** match squad and seed colors; return the first squad before changing colors.
- **003 — The Long Harvest:** use the amber carrier's capacity of two for the paired amber seeds, then make a separate teal trip.
- **004 — Twin Trail Crossing:** explain that both deployment slots can be used before returning squads through the narrow shared pass.
- **005 — The Busy Nest:** explain the two-slot / three-color constraint, the amber paired load, and how returning a squad rotates in the waiting color.

The tips are presentation-only and are resolved from the current selected squad, deployed-slot count, carried load, and delivered count. They appear below the level objective and do not replace action feedback, alter the board, or intercept touch input.
